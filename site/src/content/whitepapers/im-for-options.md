---
title: "A closed form model-free approximation for the Initial Margin of option portfolios"
date: 2023-06-01
pdf: /whitepapers/zwp-012-IMforOptions.pdf
ref: zwp-012
---

In this article we analyze the state of the art for computing initial margins for options, whose core component is generally based on a VaR or Expected Shortfall risk measure. We derive an approximation formula for the VaR at short horizons in a model-free setting. This innovating formula has promising features and behaves in a much more satisfactory way than the classical Filtered Historical Simulation-based VaR in our numerical experiments.
