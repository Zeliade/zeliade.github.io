Paolo joined the Zeliade Systems in 2025, where he works on quantitative risk analysis, modeling, stress testing and clearing of digital assets. He is currently completing a Master's degree (M2) in Applied Mathematics, Financial Mathematics track, at Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne, after earning his M1 in Mathematical Modelling from the same institution.

Before joining Zeliade, Paolo gained significant experience in the decentralized finance (DeFi) space, co-founding a blockchain-based B2C money transfer application that secured investor backing and received multiple blockchain innovation grants. There, alongside his CFO duties, he led the engineering team on financial technology and system architecture, focusing on the intersection between decentralized (DeFi) and traditional (TradFi) financial infrastructures.
