Zeliade combines state-of-the-art quantitative and financial knowledge along with a thorough
expertise in clearing regulations (EMIR, PFMI, US CFTC) and a global perspective on the CCP universe.
We deliver rigorous, audit-ready validation reports and support our clients through regulatory
reviews and risk committee presentations.

## Validation scope

We offer a comprehensive validation of the full range of CCP risk methodologies:

- **Initial margin models:** SPAN, Filtered Historical Simulation VaR, Monte-Carlo VaR, Stressed VaR,
  including add-ons (liquidity, concentration, wrong-way risk, sovereign risk, settlement risk,
  credit risk), backtesting, portfolio margining, procyclicality and sensitivity analysis.
- **Market data:** reliability of the sourcing and verification of market data, data cleansing steps,
  data proxying, price determination methodology, implied volatility surface modelling.
- **Pricing methodology:** pricing models' theoretical soundness, appropriateness of the risk factors
  used in pricing, numerical performance, precision and stability, yield curve bootstrapping
  methodologies.
- **Stress testing:** soundness of the stress scenarios, plausibility measures and adjustments,
  sizing, allocation and monitoring of the Default Fund.
- **Collateral risk methodology:** eligibility criteria, haircuts calibration, valuation and
  monitoring, concentration limits.
- **Liquidity risk methodology:** liquidity requirements and resources, exposure to liquidity
  providers, custodians and investment counterparties, management and monitoring of the CCP's
  liquidity needs across a range of market scenarios, liquidity stress testing and liquidity sizing
  methodology.
- **Internal credit risk:** scoring for the CCP's counterparties (clearing members, investment and
  liquidity providers, etc.).
- **Investment risk policy:** credit risk limits, concentration limits, etc.

## Markets covered

Our experience encompasses all cleared markets:

- **Cash securities:** cash equity, exchange traded products (ETPs), warrants, securities financing
  transactions (SFTs), bonds, repos, GC repos.
- **Exchange traded derivatives:** futures and options on equities, indices, bonds, interest rates,
  foreign exchange and commodities.
- **Credit:** credit default swaps.
- **OTC interest rate derivatives:** OIS, single currency IRS, basis swaps, FRAs, non-deliverable IRS,
  cross currency swaps, swaptions.
- **OTC FX derivatives:** non-deliverable and deliverable FX forwards, FX options.
